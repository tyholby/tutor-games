## Knowledge Bowl Practice Questions - Combined Set 1

### Question 1
**Q:** Which European country is a peninsula extending into the Mediterranean Sea and is shaped like a boot?  
**A:** Italy

### Question 2
**Q:** What is the capital of Norway, a Scandinavian country located in Northern Europe?  
**A:** Oslo

### Question 3
**Q:** The Golden Gate Bridge is a famous landmark located in which U.S. city?  
**A:** San Francisco, California

### Question 4
**Q:** What is the term for a narrow, deep inlet of the sea between high cliffs, typically formed by glacial erosion and commonly found in Norway and New Zealand?  
**A:** A fjord

### Question 5
**Q:** Name the three most populated countries in the world.  
**A:** China, India, United States

### Question 6
**Q:** Which strait connects the Mediterranean Sea to the Atlantic Ocean and separates Europe from Africa?  
**A:** The Strait of Gibraltar

### Question 7
**Q:** Which Asian mountain range contains Mount Everest, the world's highest peak at approximately 29,000 feet?  
**A:** The Himalayas

### Question 8
**Q:** In which country would you find Stonehenge, an ancient prehistoric monument consisting of massive standing stones?  
**A:** United Kingdom (or England)

### Question 9
**Q:** What is the nickname of Florida, the southeasternmost state known for its beaches and theme parks?  
**A:** The Sunshine State

### Question 10
**Q:** What is the term for a triangular area of land formed by sediment deposited at the mouth of a river? Egypt's capital, Cairo, is located near this feature of the Nile River.  
**A:** A delta (the Nile Delta)

### Question 11
**Q:** What is the capital of Pennsylvania, located on the Susquehanna River?  
**A:** Harrisburg

### Question 12
**Q:** What is the capital of Poland, a country in Eastern Europe that borders Germany and Ukraine?  
**A:** Warsaw

### Question 13
**Q:** Name the two countries that are located in both Europe and Asia.  
**A:** Russia and Turkey

### Question 14
**Q:** What is the term for the line that separates river systems, directing water to flow to different oceans or seas? In North America, this major divide runs along the Rocky Mountains.  
**A:** A continental divide (or the Continental Divide)

### Question 15
**Q:** What is the former name of Istanbul, Turkey, when it was the capital of the Byzantine Empire and later the Ottoman Empire?  
**A:** Constantinople
