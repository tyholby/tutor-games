## Knowledge Bowl Practice Questions - Set 2

### General Geography Terms

1. **Q:** What is the term for the triangular area of land formed by sediment deposited at the mouth of a river?  
   **A:** A delta

2. **Q:** What do we call a body of water that is partially enclosed by land but has a wide opening to the sea?  
   **A:** A bay

3. **Q:** What is the name for a narrow passage of water connecting two larger bodies of water?  
   **A:** A strait

4. **Q:** What is the term for a deep, narrow valley with steep sides, often carved by a river?  
   **A:** A canyon

5. **Q:** What do geographers call a large area of flat or gently rolling grassland with few trees?  
   **A:** A prairie

6. **Q:** What is the term for a large area of land covered with trees and undergrowth?  
   **A:** A forest

7. **Q:** What is the name for the imaginary line that runs from the North Pole to the South Pole and passes through Greenwich, England?  
   **A:** The Prime Meridian (0° longitude)

8. **Q:** What do we call the distance measured in degrees north or south of the Equator?  
   **A:** Latitude
