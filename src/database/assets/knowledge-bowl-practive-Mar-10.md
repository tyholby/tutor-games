# Knowledge Bowl Practice — March 10

**Format:** For each round, read the **Toss-Up** aloud. First team to buzz in answers. If correct, that team gets the **Secondary Question** (team deliberates together — usually worth more points).

---

## Round 1 — U.S. State Capitals

**Toss-Up:**
> What is the capital of Kentucky?

**Answer:** Frankfort

---

**Secondary Question:**
> Name the capitals of these five southeastern U.S. states: Alabama, Georgia, Tennessee, Mississippi, and Louisiana.

**Answers:**
- Alabama → Montgomery
- Georgia → Atlanta
- Tennessee → Nashville
- Mississippi → Jackson
- Louisiana → Baton Rouge

---

## Round 2 — U.S. Landmarks & Geography

**Toss-Up:**
> The Alamo — a historic mission and fortress and site of a famous 1836 battle — is located in which U.S. city and state?

**Answer:** San Antonio, Texas

---

**Secondary Question:**
> Name all four bodies of water that border the United States (including Alaska).

**Answers:**
- Atlantic Ocean (east coast)
- Pacific Ocean (west coast)
- Gulf of Mexico (south)
- Arctic Ocean (north, bordering Alaska)

---

## Round 3 — Geography Terms

**Toss-Up:**
> What is the geography term for a ring-shaped coral reef or series of islands that surrounds a lagoon? Examples include islands in the Marshall Islands and the Maldives.

**Answer:** Atoll

---

**Secondary Question:**
> Give the geography term for each of these three descriptions:
> 1. A piece of land almost completely surrounded by water — Florida and Italy are examples.
> 2. A slow-moving mass of ice formed from compacted snow.
> 3. The tidal mouth of a large river where the river meets the sea, and freshwater mixes with saltwater.

**Answers:**
1. Peninsula
2. Glacier
3. Estuary
