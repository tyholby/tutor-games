## Knowledge Bowl Practice Questions

### General Geography Terms

1. **Q:** What is the term for a chain or group of islands, such as Hawaii or the Philippines?  
   **A:** Archipelago

2. **Q:** What do we call the height of a location above sea level?  
   **A:** Altitude

3. **Q:** What is the name for the region around the North Pole, known for its cold temperatures and ice?  
   **A:** The Arctic Region (or simply “the Arctic”)

4. **Q:** What is a narrow piece of land that connects two larger land areas, often with water on both sides?  
   **A:** An isthmus

5. **Q:** What do geographers call a large, mostly flat area of land that has only small changes in elevation?  
   **A:** A plain

6. **Q:** What is the term for a river that flows into a larger river?  
   **A:** A tributary

7. **Q:** What is the term for a dry region on one side of a mountain range that receives little rainfall because the mountains block moist air?  
   **A:** A rain shadow

8. **Q:** What is the name for the imaginary line that divides the Earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres?  
   **A:** The Equator

### United States – States and Capitals

9. **Q:** Which U.S. state capital is located farthest north: Atlanta, Juneau, or Denver?  
   **A:** Juneau, Alaska

10. **Q:** What is the capital of the state of Colorado?  
    **A:** Denver

11. **Q:** Which state is known as the “Sunshine State” and is located in the southeastern United States?  
    **A:** Florida

12. **Q:** Which U.S. state borders both the Atlantic Ocean and Canada and is famous for its many lakes and the city of Augusta as its capital?  
    **A:** Maine

### World Country Locations & Capitals

13. **Q:** What is the capital city of Japan, located on the island of Honshu and one of the largest cities in the world?  
    **A:** Tokyo

14. **Q:** What is the capital of Egypt, a major city on the Nile River near the ancient pyramids of Giza?  
    **A:** Cairo

15. **Q:** What is the capital city of Australia, located inland between Sydney and Melbourne?  
    **A:** Canberra

16. **Q:** Which European capital city is divided by the River Thames and includes famous landmarks like Big Ben and the Tower Bridge?  
    **A:** London

17. **Q:** What is the capital of Brazil, a planned city in the interior of the country whose shape from above looks like an airplane or bird?  
    **A:** Brasília

18. **Q:** Which Asian capital city lies on the Han River and is the capital of South Korea?  
    **A:** Seoul

### World Cities & Landmarks

19. **Q:** In which European city would you find the Eiffel Tower?  
    **A:** Paris

20. **Q:** In which U.S. city would you find the Gateway Arch, a monument honoring westward expansion?  
    **A:** St. Louis

21. **Q:** Which Egyptian landmark consists of three main pyramids and the nearby Great Sphinx on the Giza Plateau?  
    **A:** The Pyramids of Giza (Giza pyramid complex)

22. **Q:** In which Italian city would you find the Colosseum, an ancient amphitheater used for gladiator games?  
    **A:** Rome

23. **Q:** Which Asian city is home to the Taj Mahal, a white marble mausoleum built by Emperor Shah Jahan?  
    **A:** Agra, India

24. **Q:** In which South American city would you find the statue of Christ the Redeemer standing above the city on Corcovado Mountain?  
    **A:** Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

### Mixed Review

25. **Q:** Which large desert in northern Africa is the world’s largest hot desert?  
    **A:** The Sahara Desert

26. **Q:** Which ocean lies to the west of the continents of Europe and Africa and to the east of North and South America?  
    **A:** The Atlantic Ocean

27. **Q:** Which line of longitude runs through Greenwich, England, and is used as the starting point for measuring time zones?  
    **A:** The Prime Meridian (0° longitude)

28. **Q:** Which continent contains the Amazon Rainforest and the Andes Mountains?  
    **A:** South America

29. **Q:** Which U.S. state is separated from the rest of the country by Canada and has its capital in Juneau?  
    **A:** Alaska

30. **Q:** Which Asian country is located partly in Europe, has its capital in Ankara, and controls the narrow straits between the Black Sea and the Mediterranean Sea?  
    **A:** Turkey
